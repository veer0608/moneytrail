moneytrail

Bank and credit-card statements into one spreadsheet — with a certificate that says whether anything went missing on the way. Every other converter can drop a row without telling you.

Drop statements here, or click to choose

PDF, CSV, XLS or XLSX · up to 25 files

Your file is read inside the one request that uploads it and removed before the reply is sent. Nothing is stored, logged or queued — there is no database, so a second visit cannot see the first one's file. The spreadsheet comes back inside that same reply and is assembled by your browser, not fetched from a server.

Prefer it never to touch a server at all? The command-line version runs entirely on your machine and is open source.
